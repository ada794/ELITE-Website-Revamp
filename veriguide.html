VeriGuide

An institutional-grade plagiarism detection and academic honesty system supporting multi-language text matching, AI-generated content checking, and seamless LMS integration for CUHK courses.

Core Features & Technological Strengths

Promoting academic honesty through advanced text matching technology, AI evaluation, and comprehensive originality analytics.

Multilingual & Cross-Script Detection

Specially engineered for English, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese text, detecting paraphrased or cross-script matching across documents.

AI-Generated Text Detection

Evaluates stylistic patterns and structural markers to help educators identify potential Generative AI involvement in submitted work.

Readability & Text Statistics

Provides readability metrics, word count analytics, character distributions, and sentence complexity evaluations alongside match ratios.

Seamless LMS & Blackboard Integration

Connects directly into CUHK Blackboard and Open edX environments for automated assignment collection and instant report generation.

Tailored Functions for Students & Instructors

Streamlined submission workflows designed for academic evaluation, peer checking, and assignment tracking.

Course Management & Integrity Oversight

Configure custom assignment parameters, set similarity threshold alerts, and review side-by-side source comparisons.

  • Side-by-Side Source View: Inspect original text juxtaposed directly with matched online, journal, or internal paper sources.
  • Custom Threshold Alerts: Flag submissions that exceed customized similarity limits for closer instructor review.
  • Batch Assignment Management: Export aggregate class reports, originality scores, and submission records directly to Excel.
  • Peer-to-Peer Copying Check: Cross-check current submissions against both historical archives and classmate assignments in real-time.
[Instructor Similarity Analytics & Source Comparison UI]

Easy Submissions & Formal Verification

Upload coursework effortlessly using your official CUHK EID and obtain certified submission receipts for academic record-keeping.

  • Official Academic Receipts: Receive a downloadable PDF submission receipt upon submitting coursework to verify timely submission.
  • Single Sign-On (SSO): Log in seamlessly using your official CUHK EID / MyCUHK credentials without creating separate passwords.
  • Draft Submission checking: Review preliminary similarity reports for draft papers when permitted by course instructors.
  • Multi-Format File Support: Upload PDF, Microsoft Word (.doc/.docx), plain text, and rich text documents smoothly.
[Student Portal Submission & Receipt Receipt UI]

How VeriGuide Works

A straightforward 3-step verification workflow for students, teaching assistants, and course instructors.

1

Submit Assignment

Students upload coursework directly via CUHK Blackboard or the dedicated VeriGuide Portal using CUHK SSO.

2

Automated Scanning

VeriGuide checks the text against academic databases, web sources, AI models, and internal repository archives.

3

Review Report & Receipt

Instructors access side-by-side similarity reports, while students download an official submission receipt.

Access the VeriGuide Portal

Log in using your official CUHK Electronic ID (EID) or explore system documentation through KEEP VeriGuide.
