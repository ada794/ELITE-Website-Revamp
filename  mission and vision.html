Pioneering Next-Gen AI Learning Solutions

Centre for e-Learning Innovation and Technology Enhancement

In response to rapid developments in artificial intelligence, ELITE leads AI-Empowered Learning at CUHK—engineering in-house AI platforms, smart assessment tools, and adaptive pedagogical frameworks.

Vision & Mission

Driving forward intelligence-driven pedagogy and digital innovation across CUHK.

Our Vision

We spearhead AI-empowered learning and intelligence-driven pedagogy. We build university capacity to harness artificial intelligence effectively, ensuring educators and learners excel in an AI-integrated academic ecosystem.

Our Mission

To serve as CUHK's innovation engine by delivering specialized AI products, customized course tutors, and adaptive learning platforms, advancing CUHK's global position as a leader in educational technology.

Our Focus Areas in AI-Empowered Learning

Delivering bespoke AI platforms and pedagogical solutions designed specifically for higher education.

Interactive AI Assessment

Pioneering multi-modal interview evaluation and soft-skill scoring platforms like Tellus to deliver objective, automated feedback.

Socratic AI Tutors

Developing domain-grounded chatbots like AcademiChat that guide students step-by-step through critical thinking rather than giving raw answers.

AI Integrity & Analytics

Enhancing systems like VeriGuide with advanced AI Writing Detection (AWD) and automated essay feedback tools (AEGIS).

AI Pedagogical Integration

Consulting with teaching teams to seamlessly embed generative AI, learning analytics, and custom course guardrails into LMS workflows.

Our Seven Strategic Aims

Driving AI innovation while supporting comprehensive university e-learning infrastructure.

AI & Digital Education Leadership

Enhance the global visibility and leadership role of CUHK in pioneering AI-empowered education.

Global Alliances & Platforms

Strengthen connections with global institutions via strategic learning networks and AI tool partnerships.

AI-Enhanced Course Pedagogy

Transform course delivery and assessment by integrating intelligent tutors and interactive media.

Smart Capacity & Infrastructure

Strengthen technical systems, AI guardrails, and data analytics infrastructure across the university.

Flexible Learning Formats

Support micro-modules, blended courses, and specialized online delivery options alongside AI platform adoption.

Lower Barrier for AI Adoption

Provide intuitive tools and turn-key AI setups so educators can easily implement smart technologies in class.

Community of Practice

Cultivate an active educator network focused on evidence-based AI teaching practices and innovation.
