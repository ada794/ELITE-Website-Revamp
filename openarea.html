Multi-Purpose Open Area

The multi-purpose open area offers a space for teachers to do preparation using AR/VR/MR, attend training/consultation, and post-production. Two iMac workstations are available for teachers to prepare their materials prior to recording/filming in the studios. Teachers can check and perform post-production editing on their materials/videos. It does not require a reservation and is available on a first come, first served basis. If you need face-to-face training or consultation services provided by Media Production Specialists from ELITE, prior booking is needed.
