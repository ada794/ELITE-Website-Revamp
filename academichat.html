Why Adopt AcademiChat?

Move beyond simple AI search answers with an interactive mentor built explicitly for deep academic inquiry.

Socratic Guided Dialogue

Engages students with probing questions and hints, pushing them to explain reasoning and identify knowledge gaps independently.

Course-Grounded Context

Instructors easily upload lecture slides, syllabi, and readings to keep the AI strictly bounded within official course scope.

Learning Milestones & Analytics

Tracks student inquiry trends and common conceptual roadblocks, providing teachers with real-time insight into class comprehension.

Automated Quiz & Schema Generator

Instantly converts existing lecture materials into tailored practice quizzes, structural layout mappings, and grading rubrics.

Designed for Instructors and Students

Tailored workflows that empower teaching teams while offering personalized 24/7 support to learners.

Transforming Course Management & Insights

Save time on routine Q&A while gaining unprecedented clarity into how your students process difficult course concepts.

  • Instant Material Conversion: Upload lecture PDFs to automatically build Socratic review guides and practice sets.
  • Customizable Guardrails: Set strict guidelines for answer lengths, allowed hints, and strict adherence to course sources.
  • Discussion Analytics: Access automated summaries and sentiment logs of student-AI interactions to fine-tune upcoming lectures.
  • LMS Integration: Connect seamlessly with CUHK Blackboard and Open edX platforms.
